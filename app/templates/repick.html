Copies this plan and swaps every item made of one material for the same kind of item in another: iron plate becomes steel plate, iron casing becomes steel casing. Hand-entered recipes are copied and swapped too. The plan you are on is left untouched, so you can compare the two.

Anything whose item changed comes back undecided, because a different item is made by a different recipe. Everything else keeps the choice you made.
