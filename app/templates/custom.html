Hand-entered recipes

A recipe the pack data does not have: a name, and a list of items with quantities. Enter a multiblock the same way — leave the machine blank, and use the block counts from the structure preview in NEI. Once saved, it is offered like any other recipe, and each block expands normally from there.

Block counts are the one thing you have to type in. The controller and the casings are ordinary items with ordinary recipes, but nothing exports the structure layout itself.
