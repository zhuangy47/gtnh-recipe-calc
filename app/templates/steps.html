One box is one trip to one machine, not one branch of the route: every branch wanting copper plates by the same recipe is the same trip, and the run count is all of them added up. Everything in a column can be done at the same time — nothing in a column needs anything else in it. A box is lit when you have everything it needs, dim when you do not; tick one off and whatever it unlocks lights up. Ticking remembers how many you did, so raising a quantity later says how many more you need rather than quietly unticking. The first column is your shopping list: nothing in this plan makes it for you, and the last column is what you asked for — the goals share it even where one could have been finished earlier, so the drawing has an end. Steps marked loop need each other and go together. Drag to pan, ctrl+wheel to zoom, wheel to scroll; click a box for what goes in and what it unlocks.
